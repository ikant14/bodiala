Severity legend: **[BLOCKER]** = must be fixed before a public, paid launch (legal, money, privacy or supplier-certification exposure). **[HIGH]** = seriously weakens conversion, compliance or operations. **[MED]** = should be fixed soon after launch. **[LOW]** = hygiene.

Go-live blocker roll-up (details and sources in the sections below):
1. There is no payment step. "Confirm booking" creates a real Hotelbeds booking straight away, with only a first and last name.
2. "My bookings" lists every customer's bookings and lets any anonymous visitor cancel any of them.
3. No email or phone is collected, and there is no confirmation page, email or voucher. Hotelbeds certification makes the voucher mandatory.
4. Child ages are never collected. Hotelbeds certification makes ages mandatory once a child count is offered.
5. Rate comments are never shown (a certification check). There is no taxes/fees breakdown or "total incl. mandatory charges" (EU CPC standard). The cancellation policy is hidden in a collapsed panel and its dates are malformed.
6. Prices disagree across the flow: results show sellingRate, but review, confirmation and My bookings show the net cost. The price-change warning can never fire.
7. `guestNationality` (Hotelbeds source market) is set to the destination country, not the traveller's.
8. The booking can be duplicated (no client idempotency key) or lost (the dialog can be closed mid-booking).
9. Accessibility failures (unlabelled inputs, dialogs that are not modal, no status messages) expose the site under the European Accessibility Act.
10. Not deployable as-is. There is no production API base and no SPA fallback config, and developer copy ("demo", ":8080", "Hotelbeds") is visible to users. There are no legal pages (T&Cs, privacy, cookies, imprint), and fonts are hot-linked from Google.

## Q1. Booking flow completeness for B2C

### Takeaway
The booking flow is a B2B "agent console" flow: it checks the rate, takes the lead guest's title and name, and confirms on the agency's Hotelbeds credit. Everything a consumer flow needs is missing: payment, contact details, child ages, a price breakdown, an up-front cancellation policy, rate comments, a confirmation page or voucher, and manage-my-booking. The current "My bookings" is a global, unauthenticated list of every customer's bookings with a cancel button.

### Inferences
- Combining no payment step with no authentication or bot protection means any anonymous visitor (or script) could create real Hotelbeds bookings on the agency's account at no cost to themselves, and cancel other people's bookings. This is the single largest go-live risk. It is a joint frontend and backend fix.
- Several B2C gaps are really API-shape gaps. The frontend cannot show taxes, rate comments, refundability per rate or a correct customer price until the backend maps them (`CheckRateResponse` / `AvailabilityResponse` DTOs and the neutral `provider.model` records). The frontend backlog should be sequenced behind those backend changes.
- The `PreBookingResponse` shape (`preBookingRequest.preBooking…`, `agentBalance`, `agentCurrency`) is a RezLive-era B2B contract ([types.ts:129-147](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/types.ts)). A B2C checkout would be easier with a purpose-built "quote" model: price breakdown, policy, comments, expiry.

### Gaps
- Not verified: whether this Hotelbeds account returns `sellingRate` / `hotelMandatory` for the target market, which decides how wrong the net-price display is in practice.
- Not verified: whether Hotelbeds' cancel endpoint offers a fee simulation mode for a pre-cancel quote. The docs page I fetched doesn't cover it.
- Whether showing the supplier name ("Hotelbeds") to consumers is allowed under the commercial contract was not found in public docs.
- PSD2 strong customer authentication requirements for the payment step were not researched here; that is the payments researcher's scope.

## Q2. Search UX

### Takeaway
Search is a B2B form: two cascading selects of supplier destination codes, native date inputs with weak validation, free-number adult/child inputs, one room, no child ages, and no `<form>` (Enter doesn't submit). Results are client-side filtered and paginated with good skeleton states, but the filter set is thin, there is no map, and on phones the filters stack above the hotels.

## Q3. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA / European Accessibility Act)

### Takeaway
Colours are deliberately AA-compliant and reduced motion is handled in CSS. The structural basics fail, though: most form controls have no programmatic label, the two dialogs are plain `<div>`s with no dialog semantics or focus management, nothing announces status changes, and route changes don't update the title or focus. For an EU consumer booking site this is legal exposure under the EAA, not just polish.

### Cited Findings
- **The EAA applies.** The European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025 and covers e-commerce services ([Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/eu-accessibility-deadline--the-european-accessibility-act-comes-into-force); [Travers Smith](https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/a-new-milestone-for-accessibility-the-european-accessibility-act-now-applies/)). Sources cite WCAG / EN 301 549 as the technical benchmark ([accessible.org](https://accessible.org/eaa-ecommerce-services-requirements/)).
- **[HIGH] Labels aren't associated with their controls (WCAG 1.3.1 / 4.1.2 / 3.3.2).**
  - In the search form each `<label>` is a sibling of its `<select>`/`<input>`, with no `htmlFor`/`id` and not wrapping the control: country, city, check-in, check-out, adults, children ([SearchView.tsx:368-437](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)).
  - The hotel-name filter input has only a placeholder and a nearby `<h4>` ([SearchView.tsx:519-527](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)).
  - Guest first and last name are placeholder-only, and the salutation select has no label ([BookHotelDialog.tsx:221-229](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/BookHotelDialog.tsx)).
  - Correct cases: the sort `<label>` wraps its select ([SearchView.tsx:578-587](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)) and board checkboxes are wrapped ([SearchView.tsx:532-540](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)).

- **[HIGH] No `autocomplete` purpose tokens on personal-data fields (WCAG 1.3.5 Identify Input Purpose, AA).** The name inputs have none ([BookHotelDialog.tsx:227-228](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/BookHotelDialog.tsx)); email and tel will need them too once added ([Understanding 1.3.5](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/identify-input-purpose.html)).

- **[MED] No status messages (WCAG 4.1.3, AA).** Alerts are `<div className="alert …">` with no `role="alert"` or `aria-live` ([SearchView.tsx:514, 590-607](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx); [BookHotelDialog.tsx:166](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/BookHotelDialog.tsx); [BookingsView.tsx:64](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/BookingsView.tsx)). "Searching…", the results count ([SearchView.tsx:574-577](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)) and "Confirming latest price…" ([BookHotelDialog.tsx:158-162](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/BookHotelDialog.tsx)) are not announced ([Understanding 4.1.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html)).

## Q4. SEO and performance for a public site

### Takeaway
The site is a pure client-rendered SPA with two routes and no crawlable hotel or destination pages, metadata, sitemap or favicon. Search URLs are crawlable query strings that trigger live supplier calls. The bundle itself is small (~70 KB gzip JS). The performance risks are the request waterfall (JS → live search → covers API → 800 px background images) and render-blocking Google Fonts.

### Cited Findings
- **[HIGH] The HTML shell is empty.** `index.html` is an empty `<div id="root">` with only charset, viewport and title: no meta description, Open Graph, canonical, favicon, theme-color or `<noscript>` ([index.html:1-12](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/index.html)). There is no `public/` directory, so no robots.txt, sitemap or favicon (repo file listing). There is no SSR or prerender; the build is `tsc -b && vite build` ([package.json:7-11](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/package.json)).
- **[HIGH] Hotels and destinations aren't pages.** The only routes are `/` and `/bookings`, and the catch-all `<Navigate to="/" replace />` turns unknown URLs into client-side redirects, i.e. soft 404s ([App.tsx:63-67](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/App.tsx)). A hotel is `/?country=…&cities=…&in=…&out=…&hotel=<code>` rendered in a modal ([searchState.ts:76-92](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/searchState.ts); [SearchView.tsx:270-272](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)). There are no `/hotels/<slug>` or `/destinations/<city>` landing pages, even though the static-data cache already holds hotel names, descriptions, images and amenities.
- **[HIGH] Crawlers and bots spend supplier quota.** Loading any search URL with no saved results runs a live availability search ([SearchView.tsx:217-236, 191-215](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx); [api.ts:81-86](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/api.ts)), and nothing marks those URLs `noindex`. The project notes the Hotelbeds test quota is 50 requests/day (bodiala CLAUDE.md). Fix direction: SSR/SSG destination and hotel pages from cached content (React Router 7 framework mode, Next.js, or Astro); `noindex` plus a robots disallow on search-result URLs; server-side rate limiting.
- **[LOW] Bundle size is fine; there is no code splitting.** The existing build output is one JS chunk of 215,852 B raw / 69,846 B gzip and CSS of 17,562 B raw / 4,215 B gzip ([dist/assets](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/dist/assets/index-rFVmtvEK.js); measured with `gzip -9`, not rebuilt). BookingsView and both dialogs are imported eagerly ([App.tsx:3-4](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/App.tsx); [SearchView.tsx:22-24](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)). `vite.config.ts` has no build options ([vite.config.ts:8-16](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/vite.config.ts)). Fix direction: `React.lazy` for the dialogs and bookings, and a size budget in CI.
- **[MED] Fonts block rendering.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Lato…&display=swap')` sits at the top of the bundled CSS ([styles.css:1](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/styles.css)). That makes three serial hops (app CSS, Google CSS, gstatic woff2) before the brand font loads. Fix direction: self-host Lato as woff2 subsets with `preload` (this also fixes the GDPR issue in Q6).
- **[MED] Images are heavy and not responsive.**
  - The backend builds photo URLs on `https://photos.hotelbeds.com/giata/bigger/` ([HotelbedsContentMapper.java:23](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala/src/main/java/com/miirphys/bodiala/provider/hotelbeds/HotelbedsContentMapper.java)).
  - Hotelbeds documents: standard (no segment) 320 px wide, `bigger` 800 px, `xl` 1024 px, `xxl` 2048 px, `original` native ([Hotelbeds – use images](https://developer.hotelbeds.com/documentation/hotels/content-api/use-images)).
  - Cards render at 260 × ≥190 CSS px (200 px at ≤980 px) ([styles.css:716-742, 927-928](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/styles.css)); the gallery is 3 columns inside a 720 px modal ([styles.css:997-999, 1157-1169](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/styles.css)).

- **[LOW] No performance monitoring.** No web-vitals/RUM or analytics code exists in `src/` (grep: the only console use is `console.warn` at [SearchView.tsx:338](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)).
- **[LOW] Asset caching is ready but not configured.** Vite emits content-hashed assets (`/assets/index-rFVmtvEK.js` in [dist/index.html](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/dist/index.html)), which suits `Cache-Control: immutable`, but the repo has no hosting config to set headers (see Q7).

## Q6. Security and privacy

### Takeaway
The React code itself is low-risk for XSS: no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, and descriptions render as text. The real exposures are the unauthenticated booking list/cancel API, Google Fonts loaded without consent, no CSP or security headers, raw backend errors shown to users, no legal or consent pages, and unsupported build tooling with known advisories (dev/build-time only).

### Cited Findings
- **[BLOCKER] Anonymous read and cancel of all bookings.** See Q1 ([BookingController.java:62-79](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala/src/main/java/com/miirphys/bodiala/booking/web/BookingController.java); [BookingsView.tsx:22-45](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/BookingsView.tsx)).
- **[HIGH] Google Fonts is loaded from Google's CDN without consent.** The `@import` sits at the top of the stylesheet ([styles.css:1](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/styles.css)). LG München I (3 O 17493/20, 20 Jan 2022) held that dynamically embedding Google Fonts without consent breached GDPR (IP transferred to Google) and awarded €100 damages, noting self-hosting as an alternative ([Universität Regensburg](https://www.uni-regensburg.de/universitaet/aktuelles/nachrichten/nachricht/04-04-2022_dynamische-einbindung-von-google-fonts-ohne-einwilligung-verstoesst-gegen-dsgvo); [DFN Infobrief Recht 3/2022](https://dfn.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Infobrief_Recht_3-2022.pdf)). The ruling triggered a wave of demand letters in Germany and Austria ([Transatlantic Law](https://www.transatlanticlaw.com/content/germany-update-google-fonts-and-no-end-sight/)). Caveat: it is a single first-instance decision ([Transatlantic Law](https://www.transatlanticlaw.com/content/germany-update-google-fonts-and-no-end-sight/)). Fix direction: self-host Lato.
- **[HIGH] No legal or consent surfaces.** Routes are only `/` and `/bookings` ([App.tsx:63-67](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/App.tsx)). The footer is developer text ([App.tsx:70-73](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/App.tsx)). There is no privacy notice, cookie/consent manager, T&Cs, imprint/legal notice, or contact/complaints page.
- **[MED] No CSP or security headers.** None in `index.html` ([index.html:1-12](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/index.html)) and no hosting config in the repo. Fix direction: a CSP (`default-src 'self'; img-src 'self' https://photos.hotelbeds.com; connect-src 'self'` plus PSP domains; `frame-ancestors 'none'` against clickjacking of checkout), HSTS, Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy, set at the CDN or reverse proxy.
- **[LOW] XSS surface is small.**
  - No `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, `innerHTML` or `eval` in `src/` (grep).
  - The hotel description renders as a text node ([HotelDetailDialog.tsx:137-141](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/HotelDetailDialog.tsx)), so React escapes it. Any markup in supplier text would show as literal tags rather than run.
  - Image URLs are interpolated into CSS `url("…")` ([SearchView.tsx:637](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx); [HotelDetailDialog.tsx:101](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/HotelDetailDialog.tsx)). A `"` in a URL could break out of `url()`, but only within that style property.

- **[MED] Internals leak in user-facing errors.** The backend `detail`/`error`/`message` is shown verbatim with the HTTP status ([api.ts:35-38](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/api.ts); [SearchView.tsx:70-71](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/components/SearchView.tsx)). Fix direction: map error codes to user-facing copy and log details server-side with a correlation ID.
- **[MED] No CSRF or bot defence on state-changing calls.** `book` and `cancel` are plain `fetch` POSTs with no CSRF token, captcha or rate-limit handling ([api.ts:99-109](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/src/api.ts)).   - PostCSS CVE-2026-41305 (stringify XSS) affects versions before 8.5.10, so 8.5.16 is not affected ([GitLab advisory](https://advisories.gitlab.com/npm/postcss/CVE-2026-41305/)).
  - Production runtime dependencies are only react, react-dom and react-router-dom ([package.json:12-16](file:///C:/Users/user/IdeaProjects/bodiala-web/package.json)).
